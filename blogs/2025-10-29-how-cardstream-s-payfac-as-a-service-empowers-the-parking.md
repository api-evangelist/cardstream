The parking industry is evolving rapidly. From traditional cash and ticket machines to mobile apps, contactless payments, and the management of Penalty Charge Notices (PCNs), customers now expect a smooth, digital-first experience when paying for parking. For merchants, the challenge is to meet these expectations while managing costs, compliance, and increasingly complex payment infrastructures.
